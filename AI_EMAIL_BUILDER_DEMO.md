# AI Email Builder Demo - JME FIT

## Overview
The AI Email Builder is a revolutionary tool that generates complete, professional emails from simple prompts. It combines:
- **Wireframe Templates**: Pre-designed email skeletons
- **AI Content Generation**: Smart content creation based on prompts
- **Visual Builder**: Drag-and-drop wireframe construction
- **Code Editor**: Full HTML/CSS editing capabilities

## Key Features

### 1. Prompt-to-Email Generation
Simply describe what you want, and the AI creates a complete email:

**Example Prompts:**
- "Create a Black Friday sale email for 30% off all training programs"
- "Welcome email for new nutrition clients with their first week checklist"
- "SHRED challenge reminder - 3 days left to register"

### 2. Wireframe Templates
Pre-built structures that AI enhances:
- **Hero Banner**: Eye-catching header with CTA
- **Newsletter**: Multi-section content layout
- **Promotional**: Product-focused with urgency
- **Transactional**: Order confirmations, receipts
- **Welcome Series**: Onboarding sequences
- **Abandoned Cart**: Recovery campaigns

### 3. Visual Wireframe Builder
Drag-and-drop components:
- Headers & Logos
- Hero Images
- Text Blocks
- Buttons & CTAs
- Product Cards
- Social Links
- Dividers
- Testimonials
- Countdown Timers

### 4. AI Customization Options
- **Tone**: Professional, Friendly, Motivational, Urgent
- **Length**: Brief, Standard, Detailed
- **Personalization**: Dynamic fields for names, programs, dates
- **Brand Voice**: Automatically uses JME FIT's fitness-focused language

### 5. Integrated Code Editor
- Live HTML/CSS editing
- Syntax highlighting
- Real-time preview
- Export capabilities

## How It Works

### Step 1: Choose Your Path
1. **Quick Generate**: Enter a prompt → Get instant email
2. **Template Start**: Pick a wireframe → AI fills content
3. **Custom Build**: Create wireframe → AI enhances

### Step 2: AI Generation
The AI:
- Analyzes your prompt
- Selects appropriate template
- Generates branded content
- Adds personalization
- Optimizes for mobile

### Step 3: Refinement
- Visual editing
- Code tweaking
- A/B variants
- Preview across devices

4. Start creating!
